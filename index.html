Our Story

A house built on memory, opened to the future

The history

Our story began in 1999 with Hotel Jhankar & Restaurant and a single promise: every guest would be known by name. Over 27 years that promise grew into a reputation, carried by word of mouth from one returning family to the next.

The vision

Ladesar is the next chapter: 10 rooms and a pure vegetarian restaurant with its own dining area, opening on 21 October 2026. It is built in the spirit of palace architecture, with carved arches and lantern-lit spaces, and it quietly adds what today's guest needs: calm rooms, thoughtful technology, easy on-site parking and a kitchen that honours royal recipes without being bound by them.

  • Living heritage

    Restored by local artisans using lime plaster, sandstone and hand-painted detail.

  • Personal service

    A dedicated host for every stay, who learns your preferences and remembers them.

  • Modern comfort

    Quiet climate control, fast Wi-Fi and deep, well-made beds behind centuries-old walls.

Pure Vegetarian Restaurant

Recipes from the royal kitchens

A pure vegetarian kitchen serving royal Rajasthani and North Indian cooking from the heart of Shekhawati, slow-simmered over wood fire and served in our restaurant and dining area. Our chefs work from family recipes and seasonal produce from the farms around us.

Ker Sangri

Desert beans and berries of the Thar, dried in the sun and tempered with mustard oil and red chilli: a Shekhawati staple.

Dal Baati Churma

Wood-fired wheat baati, five-lentil dal and sweet churma, finished at your table with a generous pour of ghee.

Ghevar with Rabri

The honeycombed festival sweet of Rajasthan, crowned with saffron-scented rabri and slivers of pistachio.
